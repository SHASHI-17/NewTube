## 🌸 How to Use Zote's Bot 🌸

### 💫 Start the Bot:
1. Open your terminal/command prompt
2. Type: `npm start`
3. Press Enter
4. The bot comes alive with love! ✨

### 🎵 Beautiful Music:
- Put your favorite songs in the `songs` folder
- Music plays while the bot works for you
- When work is done, music rests

### 📱 In Telegram:
- Send `/start` to begin
- Send `/tweet` to share a tweet
- Tap the beautiful buttons to choose what to do
- Click "START" and watch the magic happen

### 💖 What It Does For You:
- ❤️ Likes tweets with love
- 🔖 Saves tweets with care
- 💕 Quotes tweets beautifully
- 🌸 Retweets tweets gracefully
- ✨ Does everything at once if you want

### 🌷 Features Made With Love:
- Multiple accounts work together
- Beautiful music plays while it works
- Messages you with sweet updates
- Never gets confused
- Always gentle and kind
